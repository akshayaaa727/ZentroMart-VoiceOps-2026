1. pol_cancellations.md — [CAN-1] Before the order ships
## [CAN-1] Before the order ships
Any order can be cancelled free of charge until it is shipped, by the customer from My Orders in the app. The support assistant cannot cancel orders itself; it guides the customer to My Orders. The refund is initiated immediately.

2. pol_marketplace_sellers.md — [MKS-1] Who ships your order
## [MKS-1] Who ships your order
Each product in the catalog has a `fulfilled_by` field. Items are either fulfilled by ZentroMart or by an independent marketplace seller.

3. help_track_order.md — [TRK-2] Delivered but not received
## [TRK-2] Delivered but not received
If an order shows Delivered but the customer has not received it, check with family, neighbours or the building security, then report it within 48 hours as a missing item under DWM-1.

4. pol_cancellations.md — [CAN-2] After the order ships
## [CAN-2] After the order ships
A shipped order cannot be cancelled. The customer can:
- refuse the parcel at the door. For cash-on-delivery orders nothing is charged. For prepaid orders the refund follows REF-1 once the parcel returns to the warehouse; or
- accept the delivery and return the item within its return window, if the category is returnable.

5. pol_coupons_offers.md — [CPN-1] One coupon per order
## [CPN-1] One coupon per order
Only 1 coupon can be applied per order. Coupons cannot be combined, added after the order is placed, or exchanged for cash.
